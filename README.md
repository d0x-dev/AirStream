# AirStream

**A beautiful, private, and deeply customizable YouTube client for Android.**

AirStream delivers an entirely ad-free, deeply polished video viewing experience. Built with privacy in mind and utilizing the Piped API, it completely eliminates tracking and ads while offering a beautiful, modern, iOS-style interface. 

[**Download Latest APK**](https://github.com/d0x-dev/AirStream/releases/latest)

</div>

---

## ✨ Features

* 🚫 **Zero Ads & Tracking:** Enjoy uninterrupted playback. No YouTube ads, no tracking scripts, no bloat.
* 🛡️ **SponsorBlock Integration:** Automatically skip sponsor segments, intros, and outros just like on the web.
* 🎨 **Stunning UI/UX:** A massive design overhaul featuring gorgeous iOS-style grouped cards, smooth animations, and a sleek custom splash screen.
* 🌙 **Dynamic Theming:** Full support for beautiful Dark & Light modes, adapting perfectly to your system preferences.
* 📥 **Downloads & Offline Playback:** Save your favorite videos and audio natively for offline listening.
* 🎧 **Background Play:** Listen to music and podcasts with your screen off, effortlessly.
* ☁️ **Decentralized Backend:** Powered by the privacy-focused [Piped](https://github.com/TeamPiped/Piped) API. Switch instances anytime you like!
* 🔧 **Highly Customizable:** Tweak the player, appearance, backup settings, and more in our brand new elegant settings menus.

## 🚀 Installation

1. Go to the [Releases](https://github.com/d0x-dev/AirStream/releases/latest) page.
2. Download the latest pp-release.apk.
3. Install the APK on your Android device. *(You may need to enable "Install from unknown sources" in your settings).*
4. Enjoy ad-free viewing!

## 🛠️ Built With
- **Kotlin** & **Android Jetpack**
- **Piped API** for privacy-first YouTube data scraping
- **ExoPlayer** for smooth, seamless media playback
- Based on the incredible foundations of LibreTube, heavily modified for UI perfection.
